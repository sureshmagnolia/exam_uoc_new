Nominal Roll Extractor (V10)

Upload multiple Nominal Roll PDFs at once, assign to rooms based on custom capacities, and print reports.

How to Use:

  1. (First Time) Go to **Room Settings** and check capacities. Save if needed.
  2. Click 'Select PDF Files' and choose 1 or 100+ PDF files.
  3. Click 'Run Batch Extraction'. This now uses your saved room settings.
  4. Click 'Download Combined_Nominal_Roll.csv' for all data.
  5. Click 'Generate Room-wise Report' for seating lists.
  6. Go to **Reports** to generate the "Question Paper Count" report.

Processing Log:

Waiting for file...

Room Capacity Settings

Define the number of students per room. This list is automatically saved to your browser. The Python script will use this list in order, from top to bottom, for allocation.

Additional Reports

Generate reports based on the last batch extraction.
